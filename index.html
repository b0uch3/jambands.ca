A gathering place for music lovers

The music
goes on.

Since 2002, Jambands.ca brought people together to talk about shows, share discoveries and connect over live music. Over more than two decades, it became a gathering place for friendships, debates, recommendations, road stories and countless shared musical moments. The forum has come to a close, but its legacy lives on. This archive preserves a piece of that history and celebrates the people, conversations and memories that made Jambands.ca a community.

Thanks for being part of it.
